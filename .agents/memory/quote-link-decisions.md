---
name: Online quote response safety
description: Commercial review and link lifecycle decisions for client approvals.
---

Only reviewed commercial quotes are shareable. Legacy material-only quotes must be explicitly rebuilt and reviewed first; a "saved quote" alone is not sufficient for safe client approval.

**Why:** Online acceptance must not bypass contractor verification of pricing, scope or customer details, or approve reconstructed legacy totals without review.

**How to apply:** Preserve this review gate across web, mobile and future email-sharing features. Editing commercial content or re-reviewing revokes earlier client links; generating another link deliberately replaces the previous link. Accepted records remain immutable.
