**How to apply:** Do not let broad Pro work gate the pilot. Prioritise reviewed business/website knowledge; named receptionist setup; complete messages and confirmed caller name/callback number; work/site/postcode/urgency/availability; emergency flags without attendance promises; partial hang-up retention; reviewed email drafts; genuine configured delivery; durable retry/idempotency controls and visible failures; availability-aware suggestions or owner-approved bookings; UK times and formats.

Real incoming call, real inbound email, actual outbound confirmation, correct contact/enquiry linking and correct booking are release checks, not demo labels or manually fabricated successes. Do not claim the pilot is live or competitive parity until those real paths have been verified. Phone reception must not live-transfer or forward the caller. Preserve existing business/customer data and other modules.
